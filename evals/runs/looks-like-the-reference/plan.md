# Principles shot looks like the reference — what to change and what to refuse

## Situation

The principles beat copied the reference clip’s black field plus its signature shape kit: colored circles, corner squares, and dashed tilted frames. That is exactly what research marked **do not draw** — the reference’s colored geometry, color capsules, and layout gags — not pacing or camera grammar.

## What you change

1. **Remove the borrowed graphics entirely.** Strip the colored circles, corner squares, dashed frames, capsules, and any other motifs you could point at in a reference frame. Do not “restyle” them; delete them.

2. **Keep the shot’s job and timing.** The principles beat still concludes the argument after the proof: one short line per constraint, on the same beat grid and duration (e.g. ~150 frames, one line per ~30 frames). When review says it looks like the reference, **delete those assets and keep the length** — do not shorten the breath or speed up lines to compensate for empty space.

3. **Replace the look with type on a plain field.** Show the constraints as film copy only: display type on black (or another quiet field), no shapes, no logo, no product UI chrome. Four lines is enough. Motion can stay minimal (line-by-line reveal, same headline/type rules as elsewhere); half-time under principles in the audio bed still applies.

4. **Re-render and review that shot only** until the frame reads as “our principles, our words,” not “their rainbow geometry.”

## What you refuse to put in its place

1. **Do not refill the frame with the product logo.** Repeating the mark in the corners, stamping it around the black field, or using logo layers as decoration reads as filler and violates logo discipline (logo assembles in the name shot, appears assembled on the end card — nowhere else).

2. **Do not swap reference shapes for “our” decorative geometry.** No substitute illustration kit, no branded color blobs, no dashed frames in product colors — that still traces the reference’s composition.

3. **Do not paste in UI, captures, or invented chrome** to “balance” the shot. Principles are not another product demo; they are type-only conclusions.

4. **Do not vague it away with “vibe.”** “Don’t copy the vibe” is not a fix. The acceptable shot is **type alone**; empty-looking black with readable lines is correct.

## Summary

**Change:** remove reference geometry; keep duration and beat; show constraints as plain type on black.

**Refuse:** logo wallpaper, corner stamps, replacement shape kits, or any decoration that fills space the reference used to own.
